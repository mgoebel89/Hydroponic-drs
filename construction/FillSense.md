To add automated flooding some changes are required to the board.
1. Solder a wire each to `JP2` and `JP3` on the cotrol board
2. Solder anotheer wire to any of the Ground (`G`) pins
3. Attach the Ground wire to the bottom of the upper container
4. Attach the `JP2` wire to the container wall at about 3/4 of the heigt of the shorter siphon tube
5. Attach the `JP3` wire to the container wall at a height just above the top of the cap on the shorter tube
