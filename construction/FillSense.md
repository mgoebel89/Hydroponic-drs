# Automated Flooding Instructions

To add automated flooding, some changes are required to the board.

1. Solder a wire each to `JP9` and `JP10` on the control board
2. Solder another wire to any of the Ground (`G`) pins
3. Attach the Ground wire to the bottom of the upper container
4. Attach the `JP9` wire to the container wall at about 3/4 of the height of the shorter siphon tube
5. Attach the `JP10` wire to the container wall at a height just above the top of the cap on the shorter tube
