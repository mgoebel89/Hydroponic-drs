# PH and Conductivity in hydroponic systems 

### PH Values

Ph values for leafy plants range between 5 (typically 5.5) and 7.

### Electrical Conductivity

Typical conductivity values for hydroponic systems fall between 1.500 and 2.500 microSiemens per cm (µS/cm).
Our readings are limited to ppm measurements therefore conversion rates from ppm to µS/cm and ppm to mg/L(Salts) are needed.

The following conversions are rough approximations:
1 ppm ≈ 1 mg/L. 
1 ppm ≈ 1.5625 µS/cm (640 scale) 

### Plant growth stages

Optimal PPM readings increase according to the plant's growth stage. 
These ranges can be found online.

### How to interpret value readings

Conductivity measurements indicate total amounts of salts in the water such as:

- Nitrogen
- Phosphorus
- Potassium

### Methods to adjust PH and EC

Most simple method is to dillute the nutrient solution concentration by replacing or adding more water
