# What the project is about

**DISCLEMER:** This project was first and foremost created as a workshop for the **"SMART GREEN ISLAND MAKEATHON 2025 on Gran Canaria."**

Therefore, please keep the following in mind:

-The workshop was designed for students aged 15 to 17.

-The project served as an introduction to the world of technology for students.

-Based on the two points mentioned above, the hydroponic system is intentionally designed to be minimalistic. The system was not designed or tested for long-term use, though it is fully automatic and functional.

-Since the workshop was conducted by coaches who assisted the participants at every step, the provided instructions, explanations, files, and plans may leave some aspects out or be incomplete.

-The provided documentation may contain seemingly unnecessary information that is not vital to the assembly

## This guide will show you

- How to build a hydroponic system yourself and how to apply technological methods such as:
  - ESP32 Microcontroller programming in Micropython
  - Implementing PH and EC sensors
- Essential knowledge & concepts to apply or perhaps even expand upon your own hydroponic projects  

## Prerequisites

- Soldering equipment
- Basic coding knowledge
- 3D printer
